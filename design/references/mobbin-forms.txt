Mobbin had no Apple Contacts, Things, or Uber/Lyft/Airbnb lost-item form screens (Uber returned its Help hub and Airbnb a video-verification screen), so these patterns come from other apps. The image links expire after 30 days, so download any you want to keep; the Mobbin links don't expire.

- Required fields first, optional ones collapsed: name, age, gender and last-seen stay visible, while clothing detail, extra phone numbers and notes go behind one "More details" row or "Add …" rows (Fitbit, Google Calendar, Skype).
- Unset optional fields show as a ghost chip or a grey "Add …" row, never an empty labelled box. Once filled they show their value, so the form also works as a summary of what's been entered (Linear, Todoist, Google Calendar).
- Grouped inset lists with labels on the left and values on the right, split into a few short groups (person, last seen, friend), are the densest layout that still scans quickly (WhatsApp, Slopes, Fitbit).
- Context takes one tap: location is a map pin with the address filled in from GPS, time defaults to "now", and the photo is an avatar slot inside the person card (Citizen, Alma).
- Actions go in the nav bar (Cancel/Done or X/✓) instead of a tall footer button, and phone fields open the numeric keypad and show a ✓ when valid. That saves the vertical space needed to fit everything on one screen (WhatsApp, Slopes, Grab).
