**8 POLISH MOVES**

1. **Use one row component with fixed columns (Things, Linear).** Keep 16pt side margins.
   - Case number: a 40pt leading column, 13pt medium, secondary grey.
   - Name: flexible width, 15pt regular, primary colour, one line, truncated at the tail.
   - Status: a 14pt glyph box plus a 13pt label.
   - Created: a fixed 36pt column, right-aligned.
   - Updated: a fixed 28pt column, right-aligned.

   Reuse exactly this row under "Recently updated" on the dashboard.

2. **Put colour in the glyph only (Linear, Shopify).** Use an 8–10pt dot or ring in blue or green, with the status label in neutral secondary text. Closed statuses get a grey ✓ glyph. Never tint row backgrounds or text.

3. **Treat every number the same way (GitHub).** Use tabular figures everywhere (`.monospacedDigit()` or `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so 11:02 and 9:47 line up. Created time is secondary grey; updated time is a lighter tertiary grey. Relative times read "now / 2m / 1h / 3d", never "ago". Both sit on the name's baseline.

4. **Let alignment carry the rhythm (Linear).** Rows are at least 44pt tall with no dividers between them. If you need one, use a 0.5pt hairline inset to start at the name column. Section headers are 13pt semibold, secondary colour, with 24pt above and 6pt below and no background band. Add bottom padding equal to the floating button's height plus 16pt so it never covers the last row.

5. **Make Closed a plain row, not a card (GitHub, Attio, Amie).**
   - "Closed" in 15pt medium secondary.
   - The count in a grey capsule: 13pt, minimum width 20pt, 6pt horizontal padding.
   - A 12pt semibold chevron pinned right that rotates on expand.
   - Expanded closed rows render name and status in secondary grey.

6. **Title as menu (Shopify, Amie).**
   - "All requests" in 28pt bold, then "13" in 28pt regular tertiary grey (no badge), then a 13pt semibold chevron on the same baseline.
   - Tapping opens a native menu listing each view with its count right-aligned.
   - Search and avatar are 32pt icon buttons on the right.
   - "New request" is a 50pt-tall capsule in the primary (black/white) fill with a 15pt semibold label.

7. **Stat tiles in two tiers (Substack, Shopify, Stripe, Asana).**
   - Layout: 2 columns, 8pt gutter, 12pt padding, 12pt corner radius. Use a secondary grouped fill on the grouped background, with no borders or shadows.
   - Label: 13pt medium secondary on top, led by the status glyph on status tiles.
   - Value: tabular, bottom-left. Open and Closed get 34pt semibold; the four status tiles get 22pt semibold at a fixed height.
   - Open's split bar: 4pt tall, full inner width, 2pt gap between the blue and green segments, 2pt radius, 12pt from the tile's bottom.
   - No legend: the Waiting and In medical tiles below already carry the matching glyph colours.

8. **Bars and Performance stay quiet (Shopify, Jobber, Mercury).**
   - "Open by post": each post gets a 13pt secondary label above a 6pt bar. Fill it in the primary label colour on a full-width faint grey track. Scale to the busiest post and sort descending. The count sits in a fixed 24pt right-aligned column.
   - Performance: rows, not tiles, with 32pt of space above the section. Label on the left in 15pt regular secondary. Value on the right as 17pt semibold digits with the unit in 13pt secondary ("24" + "m"). Separate rows with inset hairlines.
