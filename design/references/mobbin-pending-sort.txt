RULES FOR PENDING v9 AND STATUS SORT

1.  Stack the queues; don't switch between them (R1, R4). On phone and on tablet under 1024 px, Pending is one scroll:
    Waiting for check first, Friend coming second. The To check | Friend coming SegmentedControl goes away. Rename
    "To check" to "Waiting for check" so the section and the status use the same words. At 1024 px and up, keep the
    two-column board, with Waiting for check as the left column.

2.  Section headers are one row and carry their own count (R1, R2). The row is 44 px: status glyph (blue ring or green
    pie), label (15 px, semibold), count in gray-10 tabular figures right after the label, and a chevron at the far
    right. No caption under it. While its section scrolls, the header pins to the top. "Pending 7" stays as the total.
    Screen reader: a heading that reads "Waiting for check, 4 requests".

3.  Inside Waiting for check, the most urgent row is at the top (R6). Order: Recheck rows that are due (blue Recheck chip
    in the timer slot, where ClickUp puts "Unsnoozed"), then everyone else with the longest wait first, so the red
    timers sit on top. Needs info rows keep their place by wait time, with an amber "?" chip. They don't get a section of
    their own.

4.  Later moves to the very bottom and starts collapsed (R2, R7). The held Not found yet rows move out of Waiting for
    check into the last group on the screen, below Friend coming. Collapsed, it is one row: violet hourglass, "Later",
    count, then the next recheck time ("4:40 PM") at the right edge and a chevron. Opened, each row is compact (initials,
    age/sex, ⟳ 4:40 PM) with no buttons. The group is hidden when it has no rows. On the desk board it sits at the bottom
    of the Waiting column. (The approved recheck design put Later inside To check. That changes here so held rows don't
    sit between the two live queues.)

5.  Inside Friend coming, sort by who is waiting now, and let the glyph on each row show the stage, with no
    sub-headers (R1). Order: Friend arrived (green ✓, someone is standing at the tent), then Friend en route (teal walking
    figure, soonest ETA first), then In medical (green pie, longest first).

6.  An empty section keeps its place (R3, R4, R5). The header stays with a gray-10 "0" and a dimmed glyph, and has no
    rows and no "All caught up" line. Friend coming never jumps up into Waiting for check's spot, so muscle memory
    holds.

7.  Every header has a chevron (R1, R4). Waiting for check and Friend coming start open, and Later starts collapsed. A
    collapsed section is its header row with the count. Open/closed state is remembered per device in localStorage,
    wrapped in try/catch.

8.  One status order everywhere, and Archived always comes last (R8, R9). Sort by status is offered on All requests and
    My requests. Pending is already in status order and gets no sort control. The order is: Recheck due, Waiting for
    check, Needs info, Friend arrived, Friend en route, In medical, Not found yet (held), and then "Archived N", still
    folded and never sorted in with open rows. Within a status, the longest wait comes first. Sorting only reorders rows
    and adds no group headers; the runs of coloured glyphs already show the blocks.

9.  The phone sort control is one tap, and its label shows the current order (R11, R12). It is a single chip in its own
    row under the title, left-aligned: "⇅ Newest" in outline gray by default. A tap switches to "⇅ Status" with a filled
    gray-a4 background and gray-12 text, and the rows reorder at once. Another tap goes back to Newest. With only two
    orders there's no menu. The chip is 32 px tall with a 44 px hit area. Screen reader: a toggle button
    (aria-pressed) labelled "Sort by status, waiting for check first", and a polite live region that says "Sorted by
    status" or "Sorted by newest".

10. On desk and tablet tables, the header itself is the sort control, and only the active column has an arrow (R9,
    R10). By default, "Created ↓" shows the arrow so the order is visible before anything is clicked. Click "Status" and
    the arrow moves to it as "Status ↓" in gray-12, with rows in the rule 8 order. Click "Created" to get back to
    newest. Clicking the active header again flips its direction. There is no third click to clear (no app on Mobbin
    does that). Inactive headers stay gray-11 and show a faint ↕ only on hover or focus. Put a button inside the Radix
    Table.ColumnHeaderCell, set aria-sort on the active one, and label it "Sort by status".
