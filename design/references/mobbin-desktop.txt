Six desktop layout rules

1. Breakpoints follow Radix Themes: sm 768, md 1024, lg 1280, xl 1640.
   - Under 768 is the existing mobile app.
   - 768–1279 (tablet): the sidebar shrinks to a 56px icon rail, the list goes full width, and detail opens as a 480px sheet over the right side. The list stays visible and scrollable behind it.
   - From 1280: 240px sidebar, list and a docked detail pane side by side.
   - From 1640: the detail pane widens to 560px.
2. Widths:
   - Sidebar 240px, resizable 200–280.
   - List at least 560px.
   - Detail pane 440px by default, resizable 380–640, remembered per browser.
   - Create modal 640px max; create side sheet on tablet 480px.
   - Sign-in column 360px.
   - Dashboard content 1440px max, centred. The request log has no max width.
3. List:
   - 36px rows (Inter 13–14px) and 32px group headers that stick while scrolling.
   - Columns: status glyph 24px, ID 72px, title fills the rest and truncates, created 88px, updated 88px.
   - IDs, times and counts use tabular-nums. Times are relative ("12m") in the cell, with the exact time in a tooltip.
   - Archived/Done starts collapsed and remembers its state.
4. Keyboard:
   - Movement: J/K or ↑/↓ moves the selection, Enter opens it in the pane, Esc closes the pane and returns focus to the row.
   - Actions: C new request, / search, ⌘K command menu, S status menu, A assign, ⌘↵ submit, ? shortcut sheet.
   - Never use ⌘L, ⌘T, ⌘W or ⌘N (the browser owns them).
   - Shortcuts appear only in tooltips, on the right edge of menus and in the ? sheet. They're never written out on screen.
5. Detail pane, top to bottom:
   - Sticky header: ID, status glyph, title, status actions, ↑/↓ for previous/next.
   - Fields with a 96px muted label column.
   - Photos as a row of three 96px square thumbnails that open a lightbox with ←/→.
   - Timeline last: system events as one muted line each, notes as full rows.
   - Status changes give a 5-second Undo toast instead of a confirm dialog.
6. Dashboard:
   - 12-column grid with 24px gutters.
   - Four figure tiles in one row from 1024; a 2×2 grid between 768 and 1023.
   - The activity column is 320px on the right from 1280 and moves below the bar lists under that.
   - Bars are neutral grey (--gray-a5); only status glyphs get colour. "Updated 14:32" sits muted at the top right.
